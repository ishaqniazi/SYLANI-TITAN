A Visionary Alliance for Digital Excellence

Saylani TITAN brings together Pakistan's premier welfare-driven technical training power with corporate innovation to cultivate the next generation of global tech leaders.

Bridging The Global Digital Skills Gap

Saylani TITAN is an impactful collaboration between Saylani Mass IT Training (SMIT), an arm of Saylani Welfare International Trust founded by Hazrat Maulana Muhammad Bashir Farooq Qadri, and TAJ TITAN (Taj Institute of Technology & Applied Networks), founded by Taj Gasoline.

Rather than relying on outdated academic models, this joint initiative delivers 100% accessible, tuition-free, cutting-edge technical education directly synchronized with international market demands. Through rigorous bootcamps, hands-on lab environments, and expert mentorship, we empower underprivileged and ambitious youth across Karachi and Sukkur to transform into skilled digital creators, self-employed freelancers, and high-earning software professionals.
